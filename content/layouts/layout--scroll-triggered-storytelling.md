## 区块顺序

1. Intro hook
2. Chapter 1 (problem)
3. Chapter 2 (journey)
4. Chapter 3 (solution)
5. Climax CTA

## 设计决策

- **CTA 位置**：End of each chapter (mini) + Final climax CTA
- **配色策略**：Progressive reveal. Each chapter has distinct color. Building intensity.
- **推荐效果与动效**：ScrollTrigger animations, parallax layers, progressive disclosure, chapter transitions
- **转化优化要点**：Keep the narrative understandable without scroll-driven effects. Use progress indicator. Mobile: simplify animations. Keep DOM reading order complete; disable parallax and scroll-scrub under reduced motion. Pause scroll animation when offscreen or hidden and render each chapter in its final readable state under reduced motion.
- **关键词**：storytelling, scroll, narrative, story, immersive
- **别名（可搜到的叫法）**：Storytelling + Data|Storytelling + Feature-Rich|Storytelling + Hero-Centric|Storytelling-Driven|Storytelling-Driven + Feature-Rich|Storytelling-Driven + Hero|Storytelling-Driven + Hero-Centric
